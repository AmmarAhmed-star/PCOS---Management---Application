💡 Wellness Tips

🥗 Diet:

Choose high-fibre foods, add protein to every meal, and limit sugary drinks. Whole grains and green vegetables help with PCOS.

🏃 Movement:

Walk or do yoga for 30 minutes daily. Regular light exercise works better for PCOS than occasional heavy workouts.

😴 Sleep:

Get 7–8 hours of sleep. Late nights and phone light can disturb hormones.

⚠️ When to See a Doctor:

Missed period for 3+ months, very heavy bleeding, severe pain, or sudden weight gain / hair fall — please consult a doctor. This app is not medical advice.

🎯 PCOS — Simple Explanation

In PCOS, hormones become slightly imbalanced — this can cause irregular periods, acne, hair fall and weight gain. Regular tracking and healthy habits make it easier to manage. Track your cycle, see the pattern, and it becomes easier to explain to your doctor.
