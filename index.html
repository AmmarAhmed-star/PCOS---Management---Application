💡 Wellness Tips

🥗 Diet:

High-fibre food, protein har meal me, sugary drinks kam. Whole grains + green vegetables PCOS me help karte hai.

🏃 Movement:

Roz 30 min walk ya yoga. Heavy workout se zyada regular light exercise PCOS me better hota hai.

😴 Sleep:

7-8 hrs sleep lo. Late night + phone light hormones ko disturb karta hai.

⚠️ Doctor kab dikhaye:

Period 3+ mahine miss, bahut heavy bleeding, bahut dard, ya achanak weight gain/hair fall — to doctor se consult karo. Ye app medical advice nahi hai.

🎯 PCOS — Simple Samajh

PCOS me hormones thode imbalance ho jaate hai — isse period irregular, acne, hair fall, weight gain ho sakta hai. Regular tracking + healthy habits se manage karna aasan hota hai. Apna cycle track karo, pattern dekho, doctor ko dikhane me bhi aasan hoga.
